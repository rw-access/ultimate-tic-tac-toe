# Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe model training — final report

Status: training concluded after R16. No R17 was started.

## Bundled checkpoint

R16 is a 280-input policy/value network with 512/256 hidden units. Its ONNX
file is 1,186,321 bytes, within the 1.5 MB browser limit. R16 passed model,
encoder, and game-rule parity checks. It is now the browser checkpoint in
`public/models/policy-value.onnx`.

## Training data

The experiments generated 136,919 games in total, including 135,919 MCTS
self-play games and a separate 1,000-game raw-policy experiment. R11–R16 added
27,000 self-play games. Holdouts and arena evaluations are excluded from those
counts.

R14's first fit exceeded the configured memory limit before its first epoch.
The retry used a 700,000-position cap and retained the complete 4,500-game
addition. R15 and R16 used the same cap and completed successfully.

## Evaluation

R16 scored 55.03% against R10 across 4,096 games from 2,048 paired openings
(draws count half): 1,655 wins, 1,198 draws, and 1,243 losses. The 97.5%
interval for its score was 53.57%–56.49%.

Against the heuristic, R16 scored 98.63%, compared with R10's 97.85%, over the
paired benchmark. The estimated 0.78 percentage-point difference had a 97.5%
interval from -0.30 to +1.86 points, so it does not establish a clear gain over
R10 on that benchmark.

## Decision

R16 replaces R10 based on the paired head-to-head result, whose confidence
interval is fully above 50%. The heuristic comparison passed non-inferiority,
but does not establish a clear strength gain over R10. Additional training is
closed until a new plan is approved.
